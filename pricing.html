Questions

FAQ

Why credits?

You buy access to one infrastructure, not a seat or a bundle of APIs. A single wallet flexes across every product, so you never over-commit before you know your mix.

Are the free credits monthly?

No — the 15,000 free credits are a one-time onboarding benefit for your first workloads, not a monthly allowance. Recurring monthly credits start with the Scale plan.

Do credits expire?

Your first 15,000 free credits do not expire. On Scale, the 50,000 included credits refresh each monthly cycle; top-up credits carry over.

Can I buy more credits?

Yes — top up any time. The wallet is one balance, shared across products.

How are credits consumed?

Parser meters per page; Verifiable Knowledge meters per page verified and per knowledge operation. See the consumption table above.

Why don't you bill per token?

Because we sell infrastructure, not inference. Parser meters pages, Verifiable Knowledge meters knowledge operations — never words generated.

Can I use only Parser, or only Verifiable Knowledge?

Yes. Each product is standalone with its own meter. Start with either and add the other when the next problem appears.

Can I bring my own LLM?

Yes — model-agnostic by design. Your knowledge is an asset you own; serve it to any model you choose via API and MCP.

Do you host models?

No. Orves is model-agnostic — you bring the model, we serve governed knowledge to it.

Do you store my data?

Tenant isolation is a platform invariant, and nothing you ingest trains shared models. Scale includes a Zero Data Retention agreement; Enterprise adds custom retention.

Can I self-host?

The standard delivery is the managed API. VPC and on-prem deployment are available on Enterprise plans.

What happens when credits run out?

API calls pause — nothing is deleted or lost. Top up and processing resumes. Charging is idempotent, so retries never double-bill.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Custom, based on volume, governance, deployment and SLA. Talk to sales for founding terms.

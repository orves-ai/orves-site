Questions

FAQ

Why credits instead of a subscription?

You are buying access to one infrastructure, not a seat or a tier. A single balance of Orves Credits flexes across products, so you never over-commit to a plan before you know your mix.

Do you charge per token?

No. Parser meters pages; Verifiable Knowledge meters knowledge operations — ingest, update, retrieve, consolidate. Never words generated. Charging tokens would put us in the same category as the models; we sell persistent infrastructure.

Can I use only the Parser, or only Verifiable Knowledge?

Yes. Each product is standalone with its own meter. Start with either and add the other when the next problem appears.

Can I bring my own LLM?

Yes — model-agnostic by design. Verifiable Knowledge ends in an asset you own; serve it to GPT, Claude, Gemini or your own model via API and MCP. Swap the model, keep the knowledge.

What happens if I run out of credits?

API calls pause — nothing is deleted or lost. Top up and processing resumes. Charging is idempotent, so retries never double-bill.

Can I buy more credits, and do they expire?

You can top up any time; the wallet is one balance. The launch expiry policy is published with rates — pilot credits are not expired from under you.

Is there volume or enterprise pricing?

Yes. Talk to sales for volume terms and founding pilot pricing.

Can I self-host, or run on-prem / in my VPC?

No. Orves is delivered exclusively as a managed API. Determinism, benchmarks and certificates are guaranteed only where we run the engines — nothing to install or maintain.

How is my data isolated, and where is it processed?

Tenant isolation is a platform invariant, and nothing you ingest trains shared models. Region and residency options are covered in enterprise terms.

Are you SOC 2 or HIPAA certified?

Not yet — those are on the roadmap, and we do not claim certifications we do not hold. Talk to sales about your requirements and timeline.

Are certificates billed?

No. Verification is part of the infrastructure, not a meter — and public certificate verification is free, no account required.

Why are there no dollar prices on this page?

Because we have not finished measuring unit costs, and we do not publish numbers we cannot defend — the same discipline as our benchmarks. Rates appear at launch, derived from measured costs.

The properties

What every Orves result carries.

Provenance

Every fact traces to its exact source. “Where did this come from?” always has an answer.

Lineage

Every result traces back through every step that produced it — each hop recorded, each hop checkable.

Versioning

Artifacts and knowledge carry versions. Corrections are new entries with history — never silent rewrites.

Evidence

How it works

Emit. Record. Verify.

Every product emits a certificate for every transformation it performs — signed, hashed, bound to inputs and outputs.

02

When evidence is insufficient, Orves abstains.

Every claim carries a resolution state. CERTIFIED means the evidence is there — go read it. ABSTAINED means it is not — and no model gets to fill the gap with fluency. Orves never fabricates facts.

Questions

FAQ

Why is Certification not a product?

For the same reason you do not buy “reconciliation” as an add-on from a payments company. Certifying is how the platform works, not something you add to your cart. Every artifact ships certified — included, always.

Can I certify artifacts produced outside Orves?

Not today. Certification covers what the platform produces. If certifying artifacts produced elsewhere becomes a real need, it may become a standalone product — that decision will be made on demand, not on roadmap slides.

Who can verify a certificate?

By design, anyone — no account, no fee. The public verification endpoint ships with the pilot rollout; today, every certificate already carries what an independent check needs.

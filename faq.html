FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I have to adopt the whole platform?

No. Parse stands alone; most teams land there and add Knowledge when they need knowledge they can trust and prove, and Vault when that knowledge needs a governed, persistent home. Everything is usage-based — start where the problem is and compose as you go.

Does Orves lock me into its intelligence?

The opposite. Orves Knowledge ends in an asset you own — model-ready for any model you choose: retrieval, fine-tuning, training data. Being served by Orves is a choice, never a requirement.

What do you mean by “verifiable”?

You can verify the origin of the information, the evidence behind it, where it sits in the source document, its version and its history. Certification of every transformation on an append-only ledger — with public, account-free verification — is the designed direction and ships with the rollout.

What does Orves Knowledge produce?

Knowledge objects: claims connected to evidence, entities, relationships, versions and provenance to the span. Everything downstream (datasets, indexes, answers) is derived from them and can be re-derived and audited.

Where do benchmark numbers come from?

From pre-registered evaluations against versioned gold sets, with n and confusion matrices reported. A number appears on this site only after it survives that process — until then you see the honest state.

How is Orves delivered?

Exclusively through the API. Nothing to install, nothing to host. Thin SDKs are provided for convenience — the platform lives server-side.

Is Orves a RAG stack?

No. RAG is one thing you can build on top. Orves governs the verifiable knowledge layer that RAG stacks improvise.

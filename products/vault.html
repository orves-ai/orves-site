The problem

Knowledge that lives in pipelines evaporates.

Parsed documents, extracted facts and hard-won context end up scattered across buckets, vector stores and notebooks — unversioned, ungoverned and invisible to the next system that needs them. Storage keeps files. It does not keep knowledge.

scattered outputsno permissionsno historyno audit traillocked to one stack
What it produces

More than storage.

A governed home

Documents, Parse output, knowledge objects, the knowledge graph, versions, permissions and audit logs — organized and isolated per organization.

Evidence you can explore

Search and navigate knowledge with its evidence attached — inspect sources, versions and conflicts instead of grepping files.

Served to everything

People through the Web, applications through API and SDK, models and agents through MCP — with permissions and provenance enforced at the door.

A property, not a product

Every artifact is verifiable.

Certification runs through everything Orves produces. Verification is designed to be public, reproducible and independent of an Orves account.

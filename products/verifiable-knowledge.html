The problem

AI stacks store conclusions and discard the proof.

Most AI pipelines optimize for retrieval and generation, not durable knowledge. Months later, teams know the answer — but not why it was accepted, what changed, or which evidence supported it.

What it produces

A knowledge base that carries its own proof.

Knowledge objects

Claims with evidence, contradictions, relationships, versions and resolution states — not text. Insufficient evidence means ABSTAINED, never a guess.

Lineage and citations

Before × after

Retrieval finds text. The layer preserves why it is true.

Traditional retrieval pipeline

  • retrieves passages at query time
  • context exists only in the moment
  • knowledge is not persistently governed
  • conflicts and versions handled externally

When the answer is challenged, the pipeline has moved on.

On the Verifiable Knowledge Layer

  • persists claims and their evidence
  • preserves relationships and decision history
  • every change creates a governed version
  • serves reusable knowledge objects

When the answer is challenged, you show why.

The discipline

Nothing is advanced by narrative.

The engineering culture behind the platform, visible in the product.

Measured before claimed

A capability ships as a claim only after pre-registered evaluation against versioned gold sets. Until then, its state is shown honestly — including UNKNOWN.

Benchmarks should be able to defeat us

Ours are designed to be blind: pre-registered metrics, versioned datasets and reported n. Their purpose is to expose weaknesses, not protect claims.

Every component must keep earning its place

Capabilities are measured continuously and replaced when beaten — so you are never locked to a single model or vendor.

Why it compounds
